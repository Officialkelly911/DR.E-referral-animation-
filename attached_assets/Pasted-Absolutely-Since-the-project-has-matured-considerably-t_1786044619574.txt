UI Dissolve Sequence

The Forum interface should gradually dissolve into the Dream Planet brand.

* Slight camera push-in (2–4%).

* Interface cards fade.
* Blur increases slightly.
* Orange highlights remain visible.
* Small UI elements (notification dots, icons, accent lines, glowing particles) gently detach from the interface.
* These elements drift toward the center of the screen.

The viewer should feel that the community itself is transforming into the Dream Planet identity.

Avoid hard cuts or abrupt fades.

No bouncing.

The logo should feel confident, premium, and intentional.
