Absolutely. Before writing another implementation prompt, I always like to create a Creation Guide first. Think of it as the director’s blueprint rather than the construction manual. If the creation guide is solid, the implementation becomes much easier and the final scene stays consistent with the rest of the campaign.

One thing I do want to challenge from the previous concept, though.

I wouldn’t end Scene 6 on a plain logo, referral code, and store badges. That’s how most app commercials end, and after five carefully crafted scenes, it would feel a little anticlimactic.

Instead, Scene 6 should feel like the Dream Planet universe has come full circle. The viewer has gone from discovering the app to becoming part of it.

Final Creative Recommendation

I would add one subtle storytelling touch that ties the entire campaign together:

As the Forum UI dissolves, let small orange UI elements, notification dots, and interface highlights gently drift toward the center, where they assemble into the Dream Planet logo. It visually suggests that every creator, fan, referral, leaderboard entry, and community interaction contributes to something larger. It’s understated, elegant, and gives the ending a sense of completion rather than simply fading to a logo. That’s the kind of detail viewers may not consciously notice, but it makes the commercial feel cohesive and intentionally crafted.
