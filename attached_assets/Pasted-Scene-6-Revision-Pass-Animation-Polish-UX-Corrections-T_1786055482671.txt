Current issue

Expected behavior

The feed should scroll vertically in one continuous, smooth motion similar to Instagram, Threads, or X.

Requirements:

* smooth easing
* constant velocity
* no snapping
* no frame skips
* no sudden position changes

Posts should continuously move upward.

Avoid restarting the scroll midway.

The viewer should feel that the app is genuinely scrolling through a live feed.
