One recommendation before you lock Master v2

Because you’ve extended the timing of Scene 3 and Scene 5, watch the full campaign from start to finish after the rebuild. Pay attention to the overall rhythm, not just individual scenes. If the middle of the commercial starts to feel a little heavy, you can trim half a second here or there before locking the master. A good commercial leaves the audience wanting one more second, not checking whether their popcorn has finished popping. That final pacing pass is often what separates a polished campaign from a merely functional one.
