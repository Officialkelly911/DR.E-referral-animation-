
DREAM PLANET PROMOTIONAL VIDEO

Scene 2 - The Reveal

Duration

3.0s - 6.0s

Length: ~3 seconds

⸻

Scene Goal

Reveal that the symbol on the hand belongs to Dream Planet.

The audience should now understand the brand name, but still not know everything about the platform.

The reveal should feel satisfying rather than promotional.

⸻

Story Purpose

Scene 1 created curiosity.

Scene 2 rewards that curiosity.

The viewer now connects:

Tattoo → Dream Planet

without feeling like they’ve been sold something.

⸻

Visual Direction

Continue using the enhanced tattoo image from Scene 1.

Don’t cut to a completely different shot immediately.

Instead…

Continue the slow push-in.

The camera is now close enough that the tattoo fills most of the frame.

⸻

Transition

The transition from Scene 1 should feel invisible.

No flashy transition.

Simply continue the camera movement.

It should feel like one uninterrupted shot.

⸻

Motion

Continue the push-in.

Around 106% → 110%

Very slow.

Very smooth.

Nothing sudden.

⸻

Focus

The tattoo becomes perfectly centered.

The DP logo becomes the visual anchor.

Everything else softly falls away.

⸻

Lighting

Continue the warm golden-hour lighting.

Introduce one additional subtle highlight.

The tattoo catches a small natural reflection.

Not a glow.

Not a flare.

Just realistic light moving over the ink.

⸻

Typography

This is where we introduce Dream Planet.

Instead of a large headline…

Keep it understated.

Example:

Then beneath it:

Dream Planet

Dream Planet should be larger.

Elegant.

Minimal.

⸻

Alternative:

Only display

Dream Planet

Nothing else.

Text Placement

Lower-left.

Leave the tattoo unobstructed.

⸻

Brand Color Accent

Use Dream Planet orange sparingly.

Only highlight

Dream Planet

or

underline the words.

Visual Enhancement

As the text appears…

Very subtly increase clarity on the tattoo.

Almost imperceptible.

The eye naturally locks onto it.

⸻

Audio

The AI song begins introducing more rhythm.

Not the chorus yet.

Still building.

Scene 2 should feel like anticipation.

“What exactly is Dream Planet?”

Transition to Scene 3

This is where I think we can make the video feel genuinely premium.

Instead of simply cutting to the forum interface, use the DP tattoo itself as the transition.

Imagine this sequence:

1. The camera reaches its closest point to the tattoo.
2. The orange-and-black DP symbol becomes the center of the frame.
3. The logo softly enlarges while the surrounding skin gently blurs.
4. The logo dissolves into the recreated Dream Planet app interface.
5. The interface loads with a smooth, premium animation, almost as if you’re entering the Dream Planet world through the tattoo.

This creates a visual metaphor: the tattoo becomes the gateway into the platform.
