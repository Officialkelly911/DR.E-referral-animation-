Dream Planet Promotional Video

Master Production Document

Scene 1 - The Hook

⸻

Scene Overview

Scene Number: 01

Duration: 0:00-0:03 (Approximately 3 seconds)

Purpose:
Capture the viewer’s attention immediately by creating curiosity rather than feeling like an advertisement. The opening should invite viewers into the Dream Planet experience without revealing everything at once.

⸻

Creative Direction

This scene should feel like the opening of a premium lifestyle commercial rather than a traditional social media advertisement.

The goal is to spark curiosity within the first second.

Instead of making viewers think:

“Here’s another app promotion.”

They should think:

“What’s that on her hand?”

The scene should be elegant, modern, minimal, and cinematic.

Think of an Apple commercial or luxury brand campaign where every frame has intention.

⸻

Primary Visual

Use the close-up photograph of Dr. Elizabeth Wisniewski’s hand wearing the original Dream Planet temporary tattoo from the Dream Planet launch event.

This image represents authenticity, history, and community. It connects viewers to a real moment rather than a manufactured advertisement.

The Dream Planet tattoo is the hero of the shot.

Everything else exists only to support it.

⸻

Image Preparation

Before animation begins, prepare the image with the following improvements:

Background

	•	Remove or heavily blur the visible rug and surrounding distractions.
	•	Add a soft cinematic vignette around the edges.
	•	Keep the center brighter than the outer edges.

Orientation

Rotate the image approximately 8-12° clockwise so that the “Dream Planet” text feels more level and intentional.

Color Grading

Create a warm cinematic grade.

	•	Warm daylight tones
	•	Slightly increased contrast
	•	Slightly reduced saturation (5-10%)
	•	Natural skin texture
	•	Soft highlight roll-off
	•	Deep but clean shadows

Avoid heavy filters.

The image should feel timeless.

⸻

Camera Movement

Use a slow cinematic push-in.

Instead of zooming outward, begin at 100% scale and slowly push toward approximately 105-108%.

The movement should be almost imperceptible.

It should feel as though the viewer is naturally leaning in.

No sudden movement.

No shaking.

No fast zoom.

⸻

Lighting Animation

Create a subtle light sweep.

A soft band of light should gently pass across the tattoo over approximately 1.5 seconds.

The tattoo itself should never glow or animate.

Only the lighting changes.

The effect should resemble natural sunlight moving across the skin.

⸻

Motion Style

Motion should remain understated.

Recommended motion:

	•	Slow push-in
	•	Gentle light sweep
	•	Tiny parallax effect (optional)
	•	Soft fade transitions

Avoid:

	•	Bounce animations
	•	Fast camera moves
	•	Flash effects
	•	Spin transitions
	•	Excessive particles

The scene should feel calm and premium.

⸻

Typography

Typography should be minimal.

Use only one short statement.

Avoid long marketing copy.

Recommended font families:

	•	Inter SemiBold
	•	SF Pro Display
	•	Geist

Font Color:

White (#FFFFFF)

Apply only a subtle drop shadow for readability.

⸻

Recommended Opening Text

Choose one of the following:

Option A

Every connection starts somewhere.

⸻

Option B

Creativity deserves a community.

⸻

Option C (Recommended)

More than followers.

⸻

Option D

Every movement begins with belief.

⸻

Text Placement

Position the text in the lower-left corner.

Never cover the tattoo.

Maintain generous negative space around both the tattoo and the typography.

The composition should feel balanced.

⸻

Music

The AI Dream Planet song should begin softly.

Avoid starting with the chorus.

Begin with:

	•	Quiet instrumental
	•	Ambient introduction
	•	Soft opening notes

Allow the music to build naturally.

The first major musical accent should arrive around the two-second mark.

⸻

Optional Sound Design

If the music leaves room:

Layer an extremely subtle ambient sound such as:

	•	Soft atmospheric swell
	•	Gentle shimmer
	•	Cinematic whoosh

These sounds should enhance the mood without competing with the music.

Emotional Goal

By the end of Scene 1, viewers should feel curious rather than informed.

They shouldn’t yet know exactly what Dream Planet is.

Instead, they should simply feel that they’re about to discover something meaningful and authentic.

⸻

Psychological Strategy

This scene deliberately creates an open loop, a storytelling technique that introduces an intriguing visual without immediately explaining it.

The unusual Dream Planet tattoo naturally prompts questions:

	•	What is this symbol?
	•	Why is it on her hand?
	•	What is Dream Planet?
	•	Why does it matter?

Rather than answering these questions immediately, the video encourages viewers to continue watching to satisfy their curiosity. This approach is generally more effective than leading with direct promotional messaging.

Creative Intent

The opening scene is designed to establish trust, curiosity, and emotional resonance before introducing any features or calls to action. By centering on a genuine moment from Dream Planet’s history, the video begins with authenticity rather than promotion, setting the tone for a modern, polished, and memorable viewing experience.
