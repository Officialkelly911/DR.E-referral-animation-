MASTER PROMPT

Dream Planet Promotional Video

Scene 1 – The Hook

Objective

Create the opening frame for a premium cinematic promotional video introducing Dream Planet.

This scene should immediately create curiosity without looking like an advertisement.

The visual should feel elegant, authentic, modern, minimal, and emotionally engaging.

The final image should resemble a luxury lifestyle campaign or an Apple commercial rather than a social media advertisement.

⸻

Source Image

Use the uploaded image as the primary reference.

This image contains Dr. Elizabeth Wisniewski’s hand wearing the original Dream Planet temporary tattoo that was created during the Dream Planet launch event.

Preserve the authenticity of the original image while enhancing its cinematic quality.

Do not replace the hand or redesign the tattoo.

Maintain the tattoo exactly as shown.

⸻

Composition

Create an extremely clean close-up composition.

The Dream Planet tattoo must be the visual focal point.

Keep the tattoo positioned close to the center of the frame.

Only reveal enough of the hand to establish that it is a real person.

Do not reveal the entire arm.

Maintain generous negative space around the tattoo.

The image should immediately draw the viewer’s attention toward the Dream Planet branding.

⸻

Background

Remove all distracting background elements.

The original rug and surrounding environment should disappear.

Replace the background with a very soft cinematic blur.

Dark warm neutral tones are preferred.

Use an elegant vignette around the outer edges.

The background should never compete with the tattoo.

⸻

Lighting

Simulate beautiful natural golden-hour lighting.

Soft sunlight should illuminate the skin naturally.

The highlights should be soft and cinematic.

Maintain realistic skin texture.

Avoid harsh reflections.

Avoid overexposed highlights.

The tattoo should remain crisp and readable.

⸻

Color Grading

Apply premium cinematic color grading.

Warm skin tones.

Rich blacks.

Gentle contrast.

Slightly reduced saturation.

Natural color balance.

Luxury commercial aesthetic.

Avoid HDR effects.

Avoid excessive sharpening.

Avoid artificial-looking skin.

⸻

Image Cleanup

Enhance skin naturally while preserving pores and texture.

Remove visual distractions.

Keep the image realistic.

Do not create plastic-looking skin.

Preserve the realism of the original photograph.

⸻

Orientation

Rotate the composition slightly clockwise so the Dream Planet text feels balanced and intentional.

The tattoo should read comfortably without feeling crooked.

⸻

Focus

Use shallow depth of field.

The tattoo should be razor sharp.

The surrounding skin should remain sharp enough to preserve realism.

The outer edges may gradually soften.

⸻

	•	belonging
	•	artistic expression
