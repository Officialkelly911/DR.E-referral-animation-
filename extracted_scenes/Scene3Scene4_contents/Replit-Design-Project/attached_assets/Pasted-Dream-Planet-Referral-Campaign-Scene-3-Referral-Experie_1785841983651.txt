SCENE 3 OBJECTIVE

Scene 3 is the first time the viewer enters the actual Dream Planet referral experience.

The viewer should move from:

“I know this is Dream Planet.”

to:

“I can actually participate, invite people, and benefit from being part of it.”

The Referral Home page is the hero of this scene.

The purpose is to introduce the referral system without turning the video into a software tutorial.

The scene should feel like a premium product reveal.

It should be:

* Modern
* Elegant
* Cinematic
* Minimal
* Visually engaging
* Easy to understand

Do not cram every feature into the scene.

⸻

SCENE 3 STORY ROLE

The overall campaign currently follows this narrative:

Scene 1

Scene 2

Brand Reveal

Scene 3

The viewer discovers that Dream Planet has a referral system and that they can participate.
