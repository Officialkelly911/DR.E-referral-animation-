# Dream Planet Referral Campaign — Release Notes v3.0

## Production summary

Dream Planet Referral Campaign v3.0 is the final six-scene vertical commercial. It combines the locked Scenes 1–5 campaign story with the approved Scene 6 premium CTA, ending on a readable referral invitation.

## Campaign duration

- Total duration: **56.767 seconds**
- Total scenes: **6**
- Format: **vertical 9:16**
- Resolution: **1080 × 1920**
- Frame rate: **30fps**
- Video codec: **H.264 / yuv420p**
- Audio: **AAC stereo campaign music**

## Supported platforms

The release is suitable for vertical social and mobile placements, including Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and mobile web/app campaign placements. Platform-specific upload limits and audio policies should still be checked at publishing time.

## Production summary

Scenes 1–5 were not redesigned or rebuilt for this release. Master v3 appends the approved Scene 6 to the locked Master v2 no-audio baseline, applies the campaign music once at master level, and preserves the established 1080 × 1920 / 30fps delivery specification. QA confirms clean transitions, synchronized audio, a readable CTA, and an intentional fade to black.
